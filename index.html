Bio

I am Chen Xianhe (陈先河), an independent researcher. Since autumn 2026, I have continued working with Prof. Yingzhen Li at Imperial College London and have also collaborated with Prof. Yihong Dong at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research interests include meta-learning, amortised inference, neural processes, and large language model architectures.

From 2025 to 2026, I was a research assistant with Prof. Li at Imperial College London. Before that, I earned an MPhil in Data Science from the University of Cambridge and a BSc in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Edinburgh.

Seeking PhD opportunities starting in 2027. I am particularly interested in positions in the UK and elsewhere in Europe, and I welcome research collaborations. If you would like to get in touch, please write to chen545284002 [at] gmail.com.
