Bio

I am Chen Xianhe (陈先河), an independent researcher. I earned a BSc in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Edinburgh and an MPhil in Data Science from the University of Cambridge. From 2025 to 2026, I worked as a research assistant with Prof. Yingzhen Li at Imperial College London. Since autumn 2026, I have continued this collaboration while also working with Prof. Yihong Dong at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interests include meta-learning, amortised inference, neural processes, and large language model architectures. I am seeking PhD opportunities starting in 2027 across Europe, particularly in the UK. If you are interested in discussing a PhD position or a research collaboration, please contact me at chen545284002@gmail.com.
